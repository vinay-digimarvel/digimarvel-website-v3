A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Put it in your brief and we’ll answer it in our first conversation.

01Scope

Do we need to move everything at once?

No. A useful starting point is one connected workflow with a clear owner and outcome. The first scope should reflect your priorities, data readiness, and team capacity.

Can you improve an Odoo setup we already have?

Yes. We start by reviewing what is configured, what is actually used and where your team is working around the system.

02Data & timing

What if our information is in spreadsheets?

Start by identifying which records you actually need. Review their structure and quality, test a sample import, and reconcile the results before deciding what to move.

How long does an implementation take?

That depends on the agreed scope, the condition of your data, any required changes, and your team’s availability. A timeline should follow discovery and a clear plan, rather than a one-size-fits-all promise.

03Platform, people & AI

Odoo Community or Enterprise?

We recommend an edition based on the apps you need, the number of users and your budget, once discovery is complete.

Will our team need training?

Plan for it. The most useful training follows the tasks people actually do, with time to practice, ask questions, and agree who handles support after launch.

Where does AI fit in?

After the foundation. Once a workflow runs reliably in Odoo, it becomes a dependable system of record. That is when an AI agent can help, by preparing follow-ups, summarizing status or flagging exceptions, with approval wherever it matters. We add it only when the data and controls are ready.
