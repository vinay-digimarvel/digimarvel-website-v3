DigiMarvel is a Business Transformation & Intelligent Systems Studio that helps growing businesses redesign how they operate, establish connected systems, and introduce AI agents that support and execute approved work.

OUR DIRECTION

Build the foundation.
Enable what comes next.

Our starting point is how your business operates: the work people do, the decisions they make and the handoffs between them. We identify what needs to change before deciding which systems should support it.

Then we build in two layers. Underneath is the system of record: one connected, permissioned and dependable platform, usually built on Odoo. On top of it sits the system of action: AI agents that act as your team’s interface to that record, helping people see what needs attention, coordinate the next steps and take permitted action, without learning another piece of software. Intelligence is only as trustworthy as the foundation beneath it, so we build in that order.
