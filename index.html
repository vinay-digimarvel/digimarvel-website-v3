The cost of disconnected work

Scattered data
tells half the story.

Sales figures are in one spreadsheet. Customer history is in an inbox. Stock and purchasing data sit in separate systems. Each source can look fine by itself, while together they show something quite different.

So the coordination falls to people. Someone has to remember the follow-up, chase the update and carry context from one handoff to the next. Exceptions such as a late delivery, a slipping margin or a top customer going quiet stay hidden until someone happens to look, and decisions wait until they do.

Manual consolidation adds risk.Combining reports by hand takes hours and opens the door to errors: inconsistent calculations, unmatched records and duplicate counting. By the time the numbers are ready, they can be out of date or simply wrong.

The system of record

One place.
Every answer.

Before an agent can help run anything, your business needs one dependable record of what is happening. We build that foundation around the way your business actually runs, so customers, sales, inventory and purchasing stop living in separate worlds.

We clean your data, agree who owns each step, and set up only the roles and permissions you need. Every order, request and approval has a clear status. The platform underneath, usually Odoo, stays in the background: your team works through the agent, not through menus and modules.

From first quote to final delivery, with no gaps.A quote becomes an order, and the order becomes a delivery. Everyone sees the whole story right away, with no more “let me check and get back to you.”

The system of action

Clean data in.
Approved action out.

With a dependable record in place, an AI agent can do more than answer questions. It gathers the context for a task, recommends the next step and prepares the work, such as a follow-up, a reorder or a status summary, then coordinates the handoff to the right person.

Where the rules are clear and the risk is low, it can carry out the approved action itself. Everything else waits for a person to review. The platform keeps the record; the agent is how your team works with it.

For example: an order running late.The agent finds the delayed purchase receipt behind it, drafts an update for the customer and a reminder for the supplier, and puts both in front of the order owner to approve.

Staying in control

The agent does the work.
Your people stay accountable.

An agent is only useful if you can trust what it does. We define its authority before anything goes live, and widen it only when the evidence supports it.

  1. 01

    Permissions

    The agent sees and does only what the role it works for is allowed to see and do in the system of record.

  2. 02

    Human approval

    Actions with real business impact, such as orders, payments or customer commitments, wait for a named person to approve them.

  3. 03

    Exceptions

    Anything outside the agreed rules goes to the person who owns it, with the context they need. The agent doesn’t guess.

  4. 04

    Audit trail

    Every recommendation and action is recorded, along with who approved it, so you can review what happened and why.

Authority starts with recommending and preparing. Acting within agreed rules comes later, one workflow at a time.

How we work

Start small.
Get it right.
Then grow.

An agent is only as good as the workflow beneath it, so we build in order. Each stage produces something your team can use, and the evidence for the next decision.

  1. 01 / 06

    Discover

    We map one workflow with the people who run it: owners, exceptions, delays, repeated effort and missing information. Then we agree how we’ll measure it.

    OutcomeA clear problem worth solving.

  2. 02 / 06

    Establish

    We organize the data, set roles and permissions, and configure the workflow in the system of record, connected to the tools it depends on.

    OutcomeA dependable system of record.

  3. 03 / 06

    Stabilize

    Your team tests real scenarios before go-live. We practise the new process together, work through questions and agree what “ready” looks like.

    OutcomeA process your team trusts.

  4. 04 / 06

    Enable

    We introduce the agent where it removes searching, preparation or coordination, and agree what it may recommend, prepare or do, and what needs approval.

    OutcomeA controlled agent-assisted workflow.

  5. 05 / 06

    Prove

    The workflow runs in everyday use. We measure it against the baseline, review the audit trail and learn from the exceptions.

    OutcomeEvidence from real use.

  6. 06 / 06

    Evolve

    With results in hand, we decide together what to refine, which workflow comes next and how much the agent should be trusted to do.

    OutcomeA deliberate next step.

    No big-bang rollouts. Each stage ends with a decision to continue, adjust or stop, based on what the last one proved.
